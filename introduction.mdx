DeepLabCut is a Python toolbox for state-of-the-art markerless pose estimation. Track any body part, on any animal (including humans), from standard video footage — no physical markers required. Powered by PyTorch with multiple model architectures, DeepLabCut supports both single-animal and multi-animal projects, offers pretrained SuperAnimal foundation models, and provides both a GUI and a full Python API.

## What is DeepLabCut?

DeepLabCut uses transfer learning from state-of-the-art computer vision models to enable pose estimation with surprisingly little training data. Label as few as 50–200 frames, train a model, and achieve sub-pixel accuracy on new videos.

Originally published in [Nature Neuroscience (2018)](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41593-018-0209-y) and extended in [Nature Methods (2022)](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41592-022-01443-0), DeepLabCut has been downloaded over 600,000 times and is used across neuroscience, ecology, sports science, robotics, and more.

## The DeepLabCut workflow

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create a project">
    Define your animal/object and set up a project directory with `deeplabcut.create_new_project()`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Extract and label frames">
    Extract representative frames from your videos and annotate keypoints using the GUI or API.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Train a model">
    Create a training dataset and train a deep learning model on your labeled data.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Analyze videos">
    Run inference on new videos to produce pose estimates, labeled videos, and trajectory data.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Refine (optional)">
    Identify outlier frames, refine labels, and retrain for improved accuracy.
  </Step>
</Steps>
